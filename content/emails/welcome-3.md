The most common rug regret I hear about is size, and it's almost always the same one: a 5×8 in a living room that needed an 8×10.

The rule for a living room is simple. The front legs of every seat sit on the rug. Sofa and two chairs? That's usually an 8×10. Sofa pushed against the wall with nothing else? A 5×8 can work. Measure the seating footprint first, then add six to eight inches on each side.

Bedrooms: the rug goes under the bed and extends at least 18 inches on the sides you step out on. Dining rooms: pull the chairs out and make sure all four legs stay on the rug. Hallways: leave four to six inches of floor on each side and stop short of the door swing.
