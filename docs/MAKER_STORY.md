# Maker and process story (supplier-specific, swappable)

**Status: DRAFT from audio. Not for publication. Not for the site, captions or emails.**

This is Part 9 of the brand story: the only section that depends on a supplier. Everything in "Draft from audio" below comes from two recordings Chris made on Sep 27, 2026 during a showroom weaving demonstration at a prospective supplier (recordings R3 and R5; R1, R2 and R4 are Ephesus sightseeing and are not supplier sources). Every sentence is what showroom staff **said** during a sales demonstration. None of it is independently verified. Source notes: `Barok_Process_Facts.md` and `Barok_Process_Transcripts.md` (Chris's files, not in this repo).

## Rules

- **Swap rule.** Parts 1 to 8 of the brand story (`docs/BRAND_STORY.md`) stand on their own. If the supplier changes, replace only this file and the per-rug Provenance Report fields. Nothing else changes.
- **Naming rule.** Customer-facing copy does not name the supplier, its owner, its town or its showroom. The default phrase is "our weaving partner in [WORKSHOP REGION]". A supplier is named only after the relationship is confirmed and the supplier agrees in writing. The earlier marketing plan flagged this supplier's tourist-retail model and online complaints as a brand-association risk, which is why the default matters.
- **Citation rule.** Every fact from the audio carries `[VERIFY supplier: R#, mm:ss]`. Strip the marker only when the fact has been verified by a named person, a document, or a measurement on the rug.
- **Quote rule.** The presenter's words need permission before they appear in customer copy. Until then, use them only as Chris's own paraphrase.
- **Villain rule.** Nothing here describes the supplier or Turkish sellers as the rug-shop script. The villain is a sales pattern, not a place or a people.

## Open questions for the supplier

1. Where exactly are your rugs woven? Your own workshop, village weavers, or both? (Audio: co-op members weave at home; no towns named.)
2. Walk us through making one rug from raw wool to finished rug. (Loom steps answered; wool prep, spinning, washing and finishing open.)
3. Where does the wool come from? Hand-spun or machine-spun?
4. What dyes do you use, per rug? How can a buyer tell?
5. What knot do you use, and what is the typical density on your 8x10s?
6. How long does an 8x10 take on the loom?
7. Who are the weavers? Would any agree to be named, filmed or quoted, with written consent?
8. How is each rug washed and finished?
9. Who checks each rug, and what do they check?
10. Who issues your certificates of origin? Can each rug get its own?
11. Can every rug we sell be verified as made in Turkey? (Afghan-made rugs are excluded at launch.)
12. When was the business founded, and how has it changed?
13. Name and registration of the cooperative, and what share of the sale price reaches the weaver.
